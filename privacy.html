Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · App: Lumi — Habit & Focus Sanctuary (iOS)

Short version: Lumi does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Lumi has no account system, no servers, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. We cannot see your habits, focus sessions, pet or settings.

Data stored on your device

Your habits, streaks, focus history, seeds, pet and purchases status are stored locally on your iPhone (in an App Group shared only with Lumi's own widgets). Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete all data inside Lumi, removes it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Lumi writes completed focus sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Lumi does not read any Health data. Health data never leaves your device through Lumi and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can revoke access anytime in the Health app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Lumi only asks for permission when you choose to enable reminders.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Lumi never receives your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Lumi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/ngodinhtinh12391-oss/lumi-legal/issues.
