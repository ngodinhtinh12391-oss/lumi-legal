Support

We'd love to help you and your companion.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/ngodinhtinh12391-oss/lumi-legal/issues — we usually reply within 2 business days.

Restore a purchase

Open Lumi › Settings (gear on Home) › Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the upgrade screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel the yearly plan

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lumi › Cancel. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Widgets & Live Activities

Long-press the Home Screen › + › Lumi to add the habit widget. Live Activities appear on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while a focus session runs (enable them in Settings › Lumi › Live Activities).

Delete your data

Lumi › Settings › Delete all data. Everything is stored on your device only.
